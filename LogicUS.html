Welcome to LogicUS

Computational Logic is subject that cuts across many disciplines, ranging from Philosophy to Mathematics, and with particular relevance in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence.

LogicUS is a multi-party system developed at the Department of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence of the University of Seville, that allows working with the standard techniques present in Propositional and First Order Logic (and make it easily extensible to other logics) by means of explanatory mechanisms and adequate representations for both formulas and transformation and deduction mechanisms. Although educationally oriented and self-contained, LogicUS has been designed to also integrate with third-party computational tools to address larger problems. With LogicUS, we aim to provide a tool for a fast prototyping and proofs for research at the same time that help to students' learning in any usual Logic courses.
